Built on Experience, Integrity, and Craftsmanship

Welcome to 99's Tailoring & Alterations. This business was built on a simple premise: treat every garment with the utmost care and every customer with genuine respect.

Over 20 years of professional tailoring, I've honed my craft, learned the intricacies of garment construction, and perfected the art of the fit. In 2023, I took a leap of faith and opened my own shop right here in Denver.

Starting a business from scratch presented its challenges, but my commitment to quality work quickly built a loyal customer base. We don't rely on flashy marketing or luxury gimmicks. Our reputation is built entirely on our precision, honest advice, and the satisfaction of our customers.

Whether you need a simple hem, a vintage piece restored, or a wedding dress altered to perfection, you can trust that your clothing is in experienced, capable hands.
